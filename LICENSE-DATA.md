# Licence for derived evaluation records

The derived evaluation records in this repository are licensed under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

This covers the records produced by the authors, including:

- `evidence/` (manifests, per-image scores, estimates, logs and completion receipts);
- `validation/` and `figures/data/` (recomputed metrics and figure data);
- `SOURCE_MAP.csv` and `ARTIFACTS.sha256`.

Source code (`experiments/`, `scripts/` and the plotting code in `figures/`)
is licensed under the MIT licence in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

Image identifiers and labels originate from the Shenzhen, Montgomery and
TBX11K datasets. The radiographs themselves are **not** included and remain
subject to the terms of their providers. Pretrained model weights are not
redistributed and remain subject to their own licences.
