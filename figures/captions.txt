Figure 2. External performance of the initial classifiers fitted to frozen RAD-DINO features. (A) AUROC on Montgomery (n=138) and TBX11K (n=1,794). (B) Sensitivity after transferring each classifier's Shenzhen-selected threshold unchanged. The dashed line indicates the empirical 90% development sensitivity target, not a guaranteed external sensitivity. (C) Specificity at those thresholds among TBX11K healthy (n=800) and other-illness (n=794) negatives. Rows identify the linear classifier and three separately fitted neural classifiers by training seed. Points indicate estimates; horizontal bars show 95% intervals. Panels A and B use saved percentile bootstrap intervals (2,000 resamples for Montgomery; 10,000 for TBX11K), whereas panel C uses Wilson intervals. Intervals condition on the fitted models and selected thresholds and do not include development-sample or threshold-selection uncertainty. Results describe agreement with dataset-provided labels.

Figure 3. Exploratory matched-encoder comparison and DINOv2 preprocessing diagnostic. (A) AUROC for RAD-DINO and DINOv2 with common preprocessing and linear-classifier specifications within each input resolution (224 or 518 pixels). (B) Paired RAD-DINO-minus-DINOv2 AUROC differences for the same cohort-resolution combinations; the dashed line denotes no difference. Panels A and B show 95% percentile intervals from 10,000 shared bootstrap resamples within each cohort; difference intervals use paired predictions. (C) Post hoc DINOv2 diagnostic point estimates for alternative resize-crop pipelines. HF denotes the Hugging Face checkpoint processor and TV denotes torchvision. Labels give the short-edge resize followed by the centre-crop size; all three conditions produce 224 x 224-pixel inputs. Training and external features were regenerated and the classifier refitted for every condition. No confidence intervals were estimated for panel C. The external cohorts were Montgomery (n=138) and TBX11K (n=1,794). These experiments followed inspection of earlier results and were not adjusted for multiple comparisons; the diagnostic contrasts concern complete fitted pipelines.
